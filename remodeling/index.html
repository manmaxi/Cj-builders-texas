Questions

Remodeling questions, answered.

Where do you take on whole-home remodels?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does a remodel start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

What is the difference between a remodel and a renovation?

A renovation updates what is already there, such as new floors, paint and fixtures. A remodel changes how the house works: moving walls, relocating a kitchen or bath, or reworking the layout. Many projects are a mix of both.

Should we remodel or build new?

Remodel when you like the location and the structure is sound. Building new makes more sense when the changes you want would touch nearly every part of the house. We also build custom homes, so we can talk through both.

Is it better to remodel all at once or room by room?

One job means one design, one set of trades and one period of disruption. Room by room spreads the cost over time, but it repeats the mess and can leave finishes that do not match.
