Questions

Kitchen remodel questions, answered.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost?

Typical ranges from our projects are $45,000 to $75,000 for simpler kitchen remodels and $75,000 to $150,000 for larger or higher-end kitchen remodels. The final price depends on size, layout and finishes, and is set in writing after the home visit.

Where do you remodel kitchens?

Southlake, Colleyville, Grapevine, Keller, Roanoke, Trophy Club, Northlake and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does a kitchen remodel start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

What does a kitchen remodel include?

Usually cabinetry, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, plumbing fixtures and appliances. A larger remodel also moves walls, the sink or the range, which adds plumbing, electrical and sometimes structural work.

Can I keep my current kitchen layout?

Yes. Keeping the sink, range and walls where they are is the simplest kind of kitchen remodel, because the plumbing, gas and electrical stay put.

Does a kitchen remodel need a permit?

Replacing cabinets and counters in place often does not. Moving plumbing, gas, electrical or walls usually does. The city where you live sets the rule.
