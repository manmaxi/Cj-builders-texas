Why a builder for your remodel

A remodel is building, in a lived-in house.

A custom home teaches a builder how every part of a house depends on the others: foundation, framing, roof, mechanical systems and finishes. That is the knowledge a remodel or an addition needs, because the new work has to meet the old house at every one of those layers.

It is why we take on custom homes, home additions, whole-home remodels, kitchens, custom storage and outdoor living with the same team and the same five-step process.
