Building goes well when decisions are made in order. Scope first, schedule second, finishes last.

C & J Custom Builders of Texas runs every custom home, home addition, whole-home remodel and kitchen remodel through the same five steps: a conversation, a visit, a written scope and price, the build and a final walkthrough.

The five steps

How a project runs, step by step.

Each step lists what we do and what helps on your side.

Request a consultation

  1. Step 1 · Within one business day

    Conversation

    We call you back to talk through the project, timing and budget.

    Helpful to have

    • The address of the home or lot
    • What you want built or changed
    • When you would like to start
    • A budget range, even a rough one

  2. Step 2

    Visit

    We walk the space or the lot with you, measure and talk through options.

    Worth bringing out

    • Plans, a survey or a plat if you have them
    • Photos of homes and rooms you like
    • Any homeowners association rules
    • Your list of must-haves and nice-to-haves

  3. Step 3

    Written scope and price

    A clear scope, schedule and price, in writing, before any work starts.

    What to check before you sign

    • Exactly what is included and what is not
    • Allowances for items you have not chosen yet
    • The schedule and the payment schedule
    • How changes are priced and approved

  4. Step 4

    Build

    One team and one schedule, with regular updates as the work moves along.

    The usual order of work

    • Permits, then demolition or site work
    • Foundation and framing
    • Plumbing, electrical and HVAC rough-in, then city inspections
    • Insulation, drywall, cabinetry, trim and finishes

  5. Step 5

    Final walkthrough

    We finish the punch list together before we call it done.

    What happens

    • We walk every room with you
    • Anything unfinished goes on one list
    • The list is completed
    • You get the keys back to a finished home

Questions

Process questions, answered.

What should I have ready for the first call?

The address, a rough idea of what you want built or changed, when you would like to start and a budget range. Photos, a survey or existing plans help, but none of them are required.

How long does a custom home or remodel take?

It depends on the size of the project, the lot, city permitting and how quickly selections are made. A kitchen is measured in weeks. A custom home is measured in months. You get a schedule for your project with the written scope.

What is a change order?

A change order is a written change to the agreed scope after work has started. It states what is changing and what it does to the price and the schedule, and it is signed before the change is built.

Where do you work?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.
