Planning your investment

What shapes the cost of a project.

These are the decisions that move the number most. We walk through each one with you before you see a price.

Request a consultation
DecisionKeeps cost lowerRaises cost

  • Scope

    Keeps cost lowerOne room, or one clearly defined area
    Raises costSeveral rooms, a whole house or a new build

  • Structure

    Keeps cost lowerWalls and roofline stay where they are
    Raises costMoving walls, adding square footage or a second story

  • Systems

    Keeps cost lowerPlumbing, gas and electrical stay in place
    Raises costRelocating kitchens and baths, new service or HVAC

  • Finishes

    Keeps cost lowerSemi-custom cabinetry and standard materials
    Raises costFully custom cabinetry, natural stone, specialty trim

  • Site

    Keeps cost lowerA level lot with utilities already in place
    Raises costSlopes, long utility runs, drainage or access work

Questions

Before you call.

Which areas do you serve?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home is just outside those towns, call and ask.

How does a project begin?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule an in-home consultation.

How quickly will we hear back?

We follow up within one business day of your request.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

New custom homes, home additions, whole-home remodels, kitchen remodels, custom storage (closets, pantries, built-ins and garages) and outdoor living such as decks and pool houses.
