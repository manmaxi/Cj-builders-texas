Questions

Outdoor living questions, answered.

Where do you build decks and outdoor living spaces?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does an outdoor project start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

What is the difference between a covered patio and a pergola?

A covered patio has a solid roof, usually tied into the house, so it gives shade and keeps rain off. A pergola has open rafters, which filter the sun but do not keep the space dry.

Does a deck or covered patio need a permit?

A roofed structure attached to the house usually does, and many cities also require one for decks above a certain height. Homeowners associations often want to approve the design as well.

Can an outdoor kitchen be added to a covered patio?

Yes. It needs gas, water, drainage and power planned before the slab or decking goes in, so it is easiest to decide at the design stage.
