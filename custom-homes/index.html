Questions

Custom home questions, answered.

Where do you build custom homes?

Southlake, Colleyville, Grapevine, Keller, Roanoke, Trophy Club, Northlake and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does a custom home project start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you. For a new home that usually means meeting at the lot, or reviewing the plans you already have.

What is the difference between a custom home and a production home?

A production home is chosen from a builder's set floor plans and finish packages, usually in that builder's neighborhood. A custom home is designed for one family and one lot, so the layout, structure and finishes are all decided for that house.

Do I need plans or a lot before I call?

No. Some clients call with a lot and a full set of plans. Others call with only an idea of the home they want. The first conversation is about where you are today.

What affects the cost of a custom home the most?

Size, the lot, the structure and the level of finish. A simple footprint on a level lot costs less to build than a larger home with vaulted rooms, long spans and heavy site work.
