Questions

Home addition questions, answered.

Where do you build home additions?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does an addition project start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

Is it better to build out or build up?

Building out is usually simpler when the lot has room, because the existing house is disturbed less. Building up keeps the yard, but the existing structure and foundation have to carry a second story, which an engineer needs to confirm.

Does a home addition need a permit?

Yes. Cities in North Texas require a building permit for an addition, and most also check setbacks and lot coverage. If your neighborhood has a homeowners association, its approval is usually needed too.

Can we live in the house during an addition?

Often yes, because most of the work happens outside the existing rooms until the new space is tied in. The tie-in is the disruptive stage, and it is worth planning around.
