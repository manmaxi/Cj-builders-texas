Which towns does C & J Custom Builders of Texas serve?

Do you work outside those eight towns?

Sometimes. If your home or lot is just outside them, call (817) 984-3311 and ask.

Where is C & J Custom Builders of Texas based?

In Northlake, Texas, in southern Denton County. Every town we serve is a short drive from there.
