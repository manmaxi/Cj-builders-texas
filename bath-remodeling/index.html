C & J Custom Builders of Texas remodels bathrooms in Southlake, Colleyville, Grapevine, Keller, Roanoke, Flower Mound, Trophy Club and Northlake: primary suites, hall baths and guest baths. One team handles the shower, tub, vanity, tile, lighting and plumbing. A bath is often part of a whole-home remodel or a kitchen remodel, and we can run both as one job.

What a bath remodel includes

  • Walk-in showers, glass enclosures and shower niches
  • Freestanding and drop-in tubs
  • Vanities, counters and mirrors
  • Tile floors and walls
  • Lighting, ventilation and electrical
  • Layout changes, including moving plumbing

Questions

Bathroom remodel questions, answered.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Typical ranges from our projects are $20,000 to $30,000 for simpler bath remodels and $30,000 to $75,000 for larger or higher-end bath remodels. The final price depends on size, layout and finishes, and is set in writing after the home visit.

Where do you remodel bathrooms?

Southlake, Colleyville, Grapevine, Keller, Roanoke, Trophy Club, Northlake and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does a bath remodel start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

Can a tub be replaced with a walk-in shower?

Usually, yes. The drain may need to move, and the floor may need to be rebuilt for a low or curbless entry. Both are worked out during the home visit.

Does a bathroom remodel need a permit?

Replacing a vanity, fixtures or tile in place often does not. Moving plumbing, changing electrical or removing walls usually does. The city where you live sets the rule.

Should we remodel the bathroom and kitchen together?

One plan means one design, one set of trades and one period of disruption. Doing them separately spreads the cost over time but repeats the mess.
