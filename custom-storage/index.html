Questions

Custom storage questions, answered.

Where do you build custom storage?

Southlake, Colleyville, Northlake, Trophy Club, Keller and Flower Mound. If your home or lot is just outside those towns, call and ask. See the full service area.

How does a storage project start?

With a brief phone conversation about your project, timing and budget. If it is a good fit, we schedule a visit to walk the space with you.

What is the difference between custom and modular closet systems?

A modular system is assembled from standard-width parts, so gaps are filled with spacer panels. Custom storage is built to the exact dimensions of the room, including sloped ceilings, windows and corners.

Which rooms can have custom storage?

Closets, pantries, mudrooms, laundry rooms, home offices, living rooms and garages. Any wall, corner or alcove that is not doing much is a candidate.

Can storage be added during a remodel?

Yes, and it is the best time. With the walls open during a remodel, lighting, outlets and blocking can go exactly where the cabinetry needs them.
